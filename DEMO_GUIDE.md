# Client Demo Guide — UEMS (Buildakar Innovation)

This guide walks through the client demonstration of the Unified Enterprise Management System, covering attendance (rotating + one-time QR), inventory scanning, NEMT trips, and role-based access.

## Prerequisites

1. Start the backend (Django) on `http://localhost:8000`
2. Start the frontend (Next.js) on `http://localhost:3000`
3. (Optional) Install the Flutter mobile app APK on an Android device/emulator

## Demo 1 — Rotating QR Attendance

**Goal:** Show a shared workplace QR that regenerates every 30 seconds, scanned by different employees.

### Steps
1. Sign in as **HR Manager** (`hr@buildakar.com`) on the laptop.
2. Navigate to **QR Attendance** → **Presentation Mode**.
3. Leave the **Workplace QR Display** (left panel) open on the laptop. It shows a rotating QR, countdown, branch name, and check-in action.
4. On an Android phone, open the Flutter UEMS app and sign in as a fictional employee (e.g., `driver@buildakar.com` / `admin123`).
5. Tap **Scan Attendance QR** → allow camera → scan the QR on the laptop screen.
6. Confirm attendance is recorded (success toast appears).
7. Switch the phone to another employee account (e.g., `emp@buildakar.com`) and scan the **same** QR again — it should succeed (shared workplace QR).
8. Scan the same QR again with the **same employee** — it should be rejected ("already recorded").
9. Wait ~30 seconds for the QR to regenerate, then scan the expired old QR — it should be rejected.
10. Return to the laptop HR dashboard — the new attendance events should appear.

### What to highlight
- QR regenerates every 30 seconds (countdown visible).
- Backend verifies expiry using server time (not browser/phone clock).
- Same QR works for different employees, but only once per employee per action.
- Expired QR is rejected.

## Demo 2 — One-Time QR Attendance

**Goal:** Show an employee-specific, single-use QR generated by HR.

### Steps
1. On the laptop, sign in as **HR Manager** (`hr@buildakar.com`).
2. Navigate to **QR Attendance** → **One-Time QR (HR/Admin)**.
3. Select a fictional employee, choose Check In, set validity (e.g., 5 minutes), and click **Generate QR**.
4. A one-time QR is displayed with the token and expiry.
5. On the phone, sign in as **that specific employee**.
6. Scan the one-time QR → attendance recorded successfully.
7. Scan the same QR again → rejected ("token already used" / "not active").
8. (Optional) Revoke the QR from the HR admin page before expiry → subsequent scan rejected.

### What to highlight
- Single successful redemption only.
- Rejected for wrong employee, wrong action, or after expiry.
- Revocable by HR/admin.

## Demo 3 — Medicine Receiving (with Barcode)

**Goal:** Show receiving stock via barcode scan and batch QR label generation.

### Steps
1. Sign in as **Inventory Manager** (`inv@buildakar.com`) on the laptop.
2. Navigate to **Inventory** → select **Medical Inventory** or **Resort Inventory**.
3. In the **Product Barcode Lookup** section, scan/enter a barcode (e.g., `8901234567890` for Paracetamol).
4. The product details display (name, SKU, stock, batch tracking).
5. Click **Receive Stock** on a product card — enter batch number, quantity, expiry.
6. Confirm receipt — stock balance increases and a movement is recorded.
7. Click **QR Label** on a product card to open/print a printable batch QR label (PNG).
8. On the phone, open the Flutter app → **Inventory** → **Scan Product** → scan the printed/on-screen batch QR label.
9. The batch details display (product, batch, expiry, location, current stock, status).

### What to highlight
- Barcode lookup finds products by GTIN.
- Batch tracking with expiry dates.
- QR label references an opaque lot ID (no quantity embedded).
- Scanning the batch QR retrieves current stock and status.

## Demo 4 — Medicine Issue (with Expired Block)

**Goal:** Show stock issue and rejection of expired/quarantined stock.

### Steps
1. On the laptop, in **Inventory**, select a product with batch tracking.
2. Click **Issue Stock** — select a batch and quantity.
3. Confirm issue — stock decreases, movement recorded.
4. Attempt to issue from an **expired** batch → rejected ("Batch has expired").
5. Attempt to issue from a **quarantined** batch → rejected ("not usable").
6. View **Stock Movements** table — all receipts/issues listed with timestamps.

### What to highlight
- Expired and quarantined stock cannot be issued.
- Auditable movement history.

## Demo 5 — Driver Trip (Flutter Mobile App)

**Goal:** Show a driver managing a NEMT trip from the mobile app.

### Steps
1. Sign in to the Flutter app as **Driver** (`driver@buildakar.com` / `admin123`).
2. On the Driver Dashboard, view the assigned vehicle and pending trip.
3. Tap **Start Trip** → status changes to "Started".
4. Tap **Picked Up** → status changes to "Picked Up".
5. Tap **Dropped Off** → status changes to "Dropped Off".
6. Tap **Complete Trip** → status changes to "Completed".
7. Return to the laptop HR/Dispatcher dashboard — the trip status should reflect "Completed".

### What to highlight
- Driver sees only their assigned trips.
- Status transitions are validated (assigned → started → picked up → dropped off → completed).
- Web dashboard updates in real time.

2. Navigate to **Resort Inventory** — should be denied or show no resort data.

### What to highlight
- Business-unit scoping enforced on the backend.
- Super admin sees both units; department admins see only their unit.

## Mobile App Connectivity

The Flutter app connects to `http://10.0.2.2:8000` by default (Android emulator loopback).

- **Android emulator:** Use `http://10.0.2.2:8000/api/v1` (default).
- **Physical phone:** Use your laptop's LAN IP, e.g., `http://192.168.1.X:8000/api/v1`.
- Find your laptop's IP: run `ipconfig` (Windows) and look for the IPv4 address.
- Configure in the app: **Settings → API server URL**.
- Cleartext HTTP is allowed for development only (documented in `AndroidManifest.xml`).

## Troubleshooting

- **QR not scanning:** Ensure camera permission is granted. Use manual token entry (copy the token from the laptop QR display and paste it in the phone app).
- **Backend unreachable:** Verify Django is running on port 8000 and the API URL in the app settings matches.
- **403 on inventory:** Ensure the account has inventory_manager or super_admin role and the correct business unit.
- **APK not installing:** Enable "Install from unknown sources" on the Android device.
